Example: 10 nationalised + 5 cooperative + 5 progress reports use 45 credits. Growth covers that month comfortably.

Does regenerating a report use new credits?
No. The first generation uses credits. Editing non-core fields and regenerating the same report is free.
Which fields lock after first generation?
Borrower name, property address, and bank name lock after the first generation. A report with different core details needs a new credit.
Do unused credits roll over?
No. Unused credits do not roll over to the next month.
How many people can use the plan?
Starter, Growth, and Studio support up to 5 team members. Enterprise is for larger firms.
Can I cancel or export my data?
Yes. Your data stays yours. Export or cancel anytime.
What happens after I sign up?
New firms enter the live early-access onboarding flow. If the first 50-firm cohort is full, the signup flow moves the firm into the waitlist and confirms that you've joined it. Any temporary beta access details are shown inside the app during onboarding.
