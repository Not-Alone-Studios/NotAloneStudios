The work is real. The system around it is usually not.

Most valuation firms are still running the day through chat, memory, phone calls, and scattered files. That works until volume rises or a bank asks for something urgently.

Assignments

Assignments tracked in WhatsApp groups.

No clear status. No visible deadlines. No easy handoff between staff.

Documents

Photos and documents live in personal folders.

What is uploaded, what is missing, and what was sent last week becomes guesswork.

Formats

Report formats change by bank and branch.

The right template is often remembered by one person instead of stored in a system.

Deadlines

There is no single queue telling you what is late and what needs attention now.

Billing

Payments are tracked in a notebook. Or not tracked at all.

Invoice status, pending amounts, and completed work rarely stay in one place.

Repogen deep-dive

Report generation that understands bank formats.

Repogen is the report generation engine inside Maulya. You fill in the property details, measurements, rates, calculations, and photos. Maulya produces the report in the format your bank expects.

How it works

Fill, generate, review, regenerate.

Self-service: You fill in the property details form. Maulya generates the report.

Assisted: You send basic data. We fill the detailed form for you. You review everything before the report is generated.

Changed a measurement or updated a rate? Edit the details and regenerate. Core fields like borrower name, property address, and bank stay locked after the first generation to prevent errors.
