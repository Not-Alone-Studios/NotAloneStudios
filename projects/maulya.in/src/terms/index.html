Terms notice

Terms visibility for the static public site and linked product flows.

This page provides a public-facing terms notice for the Maulya marketing site. It also explains that some authoritative terms acceptance for workspace use or updates participation may happen on linked app-host surfaces.

Public-site use

The marketing site is provided as a static informational surface for product overview, solutions, workflow survey entry, updates discovery, and entry into pilot or onboarding flows.

No operational workspace access from this notice alone

Using the public site does not by itself grant workspace access, updates-account participation rights, or contributor/admin access. Those actions can require sign-in, role checks, and current policy acceptance on the app host.

Informational material

Public updates, articles, and related discussions are informational in nature and should not be treated as financial advice. The public knowledge lane is separate from the operational workspace.

Linked flows

  • app.maulya.in currently owns workspace, updates reader, public account, and contributor/admin participation lanes
  • auth.maulya.in currently owns browser sign-in origin
  • pilot and access requests can hand users into live onboarding flows on the app host
  • the workflow survey remains a public static entry path even when live app lanes are under pressure

Changes

As the public/app integration evolves, this notice may be updated to better reflect the current hosting and participation model.

Important

Do not treat the static site as the whole product contract.

The static marketing site is a front door. Operational behavior, public account settings, participation, and some policy acceptance remain product-host responsibilities.
