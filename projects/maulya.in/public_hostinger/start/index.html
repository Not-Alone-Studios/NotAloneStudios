Start your firm

Four steps from here to your first file.

During the beta we set up each firm with its owner, so everything is right from the first day. This is how it goes.

  1. Tell us about your office

    Write to us with your firm's name, town, how many people work on files, and the banks you work with. A short email is enough.

    Write to ussupport@maulya.in

    If you have a few minutes more, the survey tells us how your office tracks work today, so we can set Maulya up around it.

  2. We set up your firm

    We create your firm in Maulya and send the owner an invitation by email.

  3. Open the invitation and sign in

    The owner opens the invitation, signs in, and finishes the firm's details.

  4. Bring your team in

    Invite each person with their job: associate or assistant valuer, office staff, site engineer or admin. Then make your first file.

Already invited?

If your firm or a colleague has invited you, sign in with the same email the invitation came to.
