Who it's for

For the whole valuation office, one person or twenty.

Maulya is for registered valuers and the people who work with them. Each person signs in as themselves and sees the work that is theirs. It is not for borrowers, buyers or brokers.

OwnerAssociate valuerAssistant valuerOffice staffSite engineerAdmin

Owner

Sees every file, the money and the team, and looks after the firm's Maulya plan.

  • Sees what needs them first on Home: reports to approve, files not given to anyone, payments late.
  • Approves and finalises reports as the registered valuer.
  • Brings the team in and gives each person their job.
  • Looks after the firm's Maulya plan in Settings.

Associate valuer

Sees every file, the money and the team. Everything except the subscription.

  • Works across all the firm's files, like the owner.
  • Checks, approves and finalises reports.
  • Sees the money and the team.

Assistant valuer

Sees every file at every stage. Makes files, gives them out, visits, enters figures and prepares reports.

  • Makes files and gives them to the right person.
  • Goes on site visits, and checks a visit sent to them.
  • Enters the valuation figures and prepares and makes reports.
  • Approves a report sent to them to check.

Office staff

Makes files, gives site visits, prepares the report and hands it over. Sees the files they make and the files given to them.

  • Enters a new file once, as it comes in.
  • Gives out the site visit.
  • Fills the report form and makes the report; the prevailing rate and the adopted rate stay with the valuer.
  • Prints, signs and hands over the report when the valuer chooses them, and records the payment at hand-over.
  • Never approves or finalises: that stays with the valuer.

Site engineer

Sees the files given to them, for the site visit.

  • Accepts or declines a visit on the phone.
  • Finds the site, meets the contact, and checks the site in steps.
  • Takes the photos the report needs; each is kept on the phone, then sent by itself.

Admin

Looks after the firm's settings. Sees no files and no money.

  • Looks after the firm's settings.
  • Brings people into the firm and gives them their job.

No picture on purpose. The admin's Maulya is the firm's settings: no files and no money.

A solo valuer, too.

Maulya does not need a big team to be useful. A valuer working alone still has files coming in, visits to make, evidence to keep and reports to find again later. Start with yourself; add people when they join.
