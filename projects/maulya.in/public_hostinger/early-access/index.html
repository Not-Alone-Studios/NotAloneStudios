What happens after you apply

You fill in the application form with your firm's details. Then:

  1. We choose 10 to 15 valuation firms

    We choose from the applications. Not every firm that applies will be chosen.

  2. We write to the firms we choose

    Each chosen firm hears from us before it starts.

  3. Six weeks, free

    Chosen firms use Maulya free for six weeks. In return we ask for real use and feedback: use it on your firm's real work, and tell us what helps and what doesn't.

Your firm's reports stay your firm's

Nothing from one firm's reports ever reaches another firm. That includes market and guideline rates, values, comparables and suggestions.
