What Maulya actually covers

Not just reports. The work behind them.

Maulya is meant for valuation firms that are tired of scattered chat, unclear ownership, missing evidence, and report workflows that only make sense to the one person carrying the whole desk in their head.

Assignments

Queues that explain the next move.

Assignments progress through visible states instead of disappearing into email chains or desk-specific handoffs.

Evidence

Uploads tied to the work, not lost in chat.

Supporting material, clarifications, and readiness signals stay attached to the assignment so teams catch blockers earlier.

Approvals

Review and release with fewer hidden blockers.

Role, approval, and readiness state become easier to understand before output leaves the system.

Reporting

Report workflows with clearer operational truth.

Repogen, regeneration, and release are treated as governed workflow, not a vague export button or side script.

Clear host ownership

Three surfaces. Three jobs. Much less confusion.

The marketing site should never pretend to be the app. This split keeps the public site stable while the live product can evolve on its own hosts.

maulya.in

Static public front door

Purpose: product overview, workflow survey, policies, pilot entry, updates discovery, and public trust.

Why it matters: fast static delivery, simpler hosting, and a public site that still works even if app lanes have issues.

app.maulya.in

Workspace and updates reader

Purpose: live workspace, onboarding, public updates reading, comments, and account/admin participation lanes.

Why it matters: workflow truth, live state, and participation remain where the product already owns them.

auth.maulya.in

Dedicated sign-in origin

Purpose: authentication and session establishment without muddying public routes or overloading the marketing host.

Why it matters: cleaner browser behavior and clearer responsibility between trust pages and access flows.
