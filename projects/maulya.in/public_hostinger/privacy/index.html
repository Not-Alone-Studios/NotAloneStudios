Privacy notice

How the public site at maulya.in handles information. It is not a substitute for the policy you accept inside the Maulya app.

What this notice covers

The public marketing and information site served from maulya.in and www.maulya.in: the product pages, pricing, the blog, the workflow survey, and the links into the app.

What it does not cover

Using Maulya itself, signing in and working in your firm happen in the app at app.maulya.in. The app has its own policy, which you accept inside the product.

Information this site handles

  • Basic visit and hosting information needed to serve the site.
  • Survey answers you choose to send through the workflow survey. They are about how your office works; the survey does not ask for your name or phone number.
  • Emails you choose to send us from the site's links.
  • An application for early access: your name, email, WhatsApp number, place, firm details and answers are used to choose early-access firms and to contact you about Maulya, and are kept for one year.
  • Visits to the early-access pages are counted with a random number kept only in your browser tab, with no cookie, and sent only to Maulya's own server. We use no analytics or advertising tracker.
  • Your move into the app when you choose to sign in.

The site sets no advertising cookies. The blog's checklists and search work only in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Questions

Write to support@maulya.in.
