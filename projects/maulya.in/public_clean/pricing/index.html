Commercial framing

This page is intentionally not part of the main public journey.

Pricing is not publicly promoted right now because packaging, included-credit posture, and report-generation tariffs are still evolving. We keep this page available by direct link as a reference, not as a decision gateway.

Why it is suppressed

We are not publishing fake final pricing. The public journey now prioritizes survey, pilot, and product clarity instead of forcing firms through unfinished commercial decisions.

Stable enough to say

Commercial posture follows operational truth.

Maulya is being packaged around role-aware platform access, workflow usage, and report-generation behavior that can be explained honestly.

Not public yet

Exact numbers and final tariff grids stay private.

We are deferring public numbers until entitlement shape, included credits, report classes, and regeneration rules are stable enough to avoid misleading firms.

Best next step

Use survey or pilot instead of treating pricing as the gateway.

The strongest current conversion path is to tell us about your workflow first, then scope rollout and commercial posture from real operating context.

Preferred path

Start with workflow fit, not unfinished numbers.
