Today, one file lives in five places.

A bank assignment arrives as a forwarded message or a phone call. Then the file spreads out. Nobody is careless; the work simply moves through messages and memory.

  • WhatsAppthe borrower's number and the address
  • An Excel sheetthe pending list, kept by one person
  • A phone gallerysite photos, mixed with personal ones
  • A laptop folderdrafts, scans and the final report
  • The valuer's memorywhich branch called, and what they wanted

In Maulya it is one file. The bank and branch, the borrower and property, the site visit, the photos and documents, the report, the approval and the fee, together, with the person who has it now.

How a file moves

Seven places, the same on every screen.

These are the app's own words for where a file is. Pick one to see what happens there, who does it, and the screen they work on.

1 of 7, Not started

The file comes in

The file is made once: who sent it (a bank, a client directly or another valuer), the bank and branch, the property and the borrower, where it is, and who does the work. You can save a draft and come back to it.

On the screen: "Documents this bank will need", "Choose the bank and the kind of property to see the documents this bank will need."

  • Owner
  • Associate valuer
  • Assistant valuer
  • Office staff

2 of 7, At site

The site visit

The site engineer sees the visit on the phone: when and where, who to meet, the site's exact location, and a check of the site in steps. Photos are taken against the list the report needs.

On the screen: "Photos for the report", "Take each photo the report needs. Each one is kept on this phone first, then goes to the office by itself."

  • Site engineer
  • Assistant valuer

3 of 7, Waiting at desk

The evidence arrives

Photos and documents land on the file, not in someone's gallery. The file says what has come and what is still missing, so the office can ask for it while the field person can still be reached.

On the screen: "Documents", "3 of 5 in. 2 missing."

  • Office staff
  • Assistant valuer

4 of 7, At desk

The report is prepared

The desk prepares the report from what is on the file: the property, the photos, the documents and the valuer's figures. The file shows who has it and since when.

On the screen: "Where the file is now", "At desk. Report being written."

  • Office staff
  • Assistant valuer

5 of 7, With the valuer

The valuer checks and approves

The report comes back to the valuer to check. Approving it is the valuer's step, shown as its own step, never assumed. Figures, approval and finalising stay with the valuer; office staff never do them.

In the app, the equals mark levels when a registered valuer finalises.

On the screen: "The report is ready to check", "Approve the report"

  • Owner
  • Associate valuer

6 of 7, Ready to go

Printed and signed

The report is ready to hand over. The file shows what is left, such as signing the printed report.

On the screen: "Ready to go", "Sign the printed report"

  • Owner
  • Associate valuer
  • Office staff

7 of 7, Delivered

Handed over, and kept

The finished report stays with its file: the photos, the documents and the history of who did what. Months later, when a bank or borrower asks about an old report, it can be found.

On the screen: "Files you worked on", "Finished"

  • Everyone who worked on it
On the computer

The desk sees what needs it first.

Home opens on "Needs your attention": files with you, files waiting at the desk, visits today, reports ready to go and payments late. Below that, the files, longest waiting first, each with one thing to do.

  • The Queue shows every file by where it is: given to you, with you, waiting at desk, at site, ready to go, finished.
  • A file's page shows where the work stands, which documents have come and which are missing, and the site visit.
  • A new file asks only what the office knows, and shows the documents that bank will need.

The whole product

On the phone

The site engineer's visit, step by step.

The visit opens with when and where, who to meet, buttons to go there and to call, the site's exact location from the phone, and a check of the site in steps.

  • Each photo is kept on the phone first, then goes to the office by itself.
  • The office sees what came, while the engineer can still be reached.
  • "Done at the site" when the visit is finished.

Who uses it

Six jobs, each with its own view.

Everyone in the firm signs in as themselves and sees the work that is theirs. These follow the app's own line for each job.

Owner

Sees every file, the money and the team, and looks after the firm's Maulya plan.

Associate valuer

Sees every file, the money and the team. Everything except the subscription.

Assistant valuer

Sees every file at every stage. Makes files, gives them out, visits, enters figures and prepares reports.

Office staff

Makes files, gives site visits, prepares the report and hands it over.

Site engineer

Sees the files given to them, for the site visit.

Admin

Looks after the firm's settings. Sees no files and no money.

What each job does in Maulya

What Maulya is not

Maulya organises the work around the valuer's judgment. It does not decide a property's value.

Not a value calculator

It does not produce a figure on its own, and it is not "AI valuation".

Not a replacement for the valuer

Inspection, method, rates, adjustments and the final opinion stay the valuer's professional responsibility.

Not a legal guarantee

It makes no claim about title, ownership or approvals.

Not for borrowers or buyers

It is for valuation offices: valuers, firms, site engineers and office staff.
