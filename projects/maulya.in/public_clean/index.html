How a file moves

From the day it comes in to the day it is paid.

One file's way through a valuer's office, in the app's own words. Each step names who does it and what they see on the screen.

Next: Give it to someone

  1. 1 of 7 · Not started

    A new file, made once

    The office makes the file as it comes in: who sent it, the bank and branch, the property and the borrower, and where it is. Choose the bank and the kind of property, and the file lists the documents this bank will need. Then the site visit is given to someone.

    On the screen: "Documents this bank will need", "Give it to someone"

    • Owner
    • Assistant valuer
    • Office staff

  2. 2 of 7 · At site

    The site visit, on the phone

    The site engineer accepts the visit, finds the site, meets the contact and checks the site in steps. Each photo the report needs is kept on the phone first, then goes to the office by itself. The floors measured on the phone are kept as the actual built-up area.

    On the screen: "Done at the site", "Who should check this visit?"

    • Site engineer
    • Assistant valuer

  3. 3 of 7 · Waiting at desk

    The visit is checked

    The visit goes to the person the engineer chose: the owner or an assistant valuer. They see what came in from the site, then accept the visit, or send it back to the site engineer to be corrected. No visit skips the check.

    On the screen: "Check the site visit", "Accept the visit"

    • Owner
    • Assistant valuer

  4. 4 of 7 · At desk

    Make the report

    The report form comes in groups, with starting answers from the file, the enclosures from its documents, and the valuer's details from Settings. Office staff can fill the whole form; the prevailing rate and the adopted rate stay with the valuer. The report is made in the bank's format, as a Word file and its PDF, then sent to be checked.

    On the screen: "Make the report", "Next: they read it and approve it."

    • Office staff
    • Assistant valuer

  5. 5 of 7 · With the valuer

    Approve the report, in one press

    The valuer reads the report: the preview, the Word file or the PDF. One press approves it, and the same question asks who prints, signs and hands it over. Or the valuer sends it back with a note. Nobody approves their own report.

    In the app, the equals mark levels when a registered valuer finalises.

    On the screen: "Approve the report", "Who prints, signs and hands it over?"

    • Owner
    • Associate valuer
    • Assistant valuer

  6. 6 of 7 · Ready to go

    Print, sign and hand over

    The person the valuer chose prints the report, signs it and hands it over, marking each step on the file. At hand-over the file shows the bill, what has been received and what is still owed, and the payment is recorded there: who paid, how, and how much.

    On the screen: "Mark it printed", "Mark it signed", "Mark it handed over", "Record a payment"

    • Office staff
    • Owner
    • Assistant valuer

  7. 7 of 7 · Delivered

    Handed over, paid, and kept

    When the report is handed over and nothing is owed, the file closes by itself. The report stays with its file, with the photos, the documents and who did what, so it can be found when a bank asks about it months later.

    On the screen: "✓ Complete"

    • Everyone who worked on it

Who uses it

Everyone in the firm signs in as themselves and sees the work that is theirs. These follow the app's own line for each job.

Sees every file, the money and the team, and looks after the firm's Maulya plan.

Associate valuer

Sees every file, the money and the team. Everything except the subscription.

Assistant valuer

Sees every file at every stage. Makes files, gives them out, visits, enters figures and prepares reports.

Office staff

Makes files, gives site visits, prepares the report and hands it over.

Site engineer

Sees the files given to them, for the site visit.

Admin

What Maulya is not

Maulya organises the work around the valuer's judgment. It does not decide a property's value.

Not a value calculator

It does not produce a figure on its own, and it is not "AI valuation".

Not a replacement for the valuer

Inspection, method, rates, adjustments and the final opinion stay the valuer's professional responsibility.

Not a legal guarantee

It makes no claim about title, ownership or approvals.

Not for borrowers or buyers

It is for valuation offices: valuers, firms, site engineers and office staff.

From Does Maulya automatically decide property value? and What is Maulya?

Your firm's reports stay your firm's. Nothing from one firm's reports ever reaches another firm. That includes market and guideline rates, values, comparables and suggestions.
