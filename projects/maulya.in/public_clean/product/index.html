Queues and attention

Work moves better when the desk is readable.

Maulya is strongest when queues, approvals, evidence posture, commercial blockers, and next actions stay visible in one desk instead of forcing teams to triangulate across tabs, chats, and memory.

  • Assignment flow designed for operators, reviewers, and field users
  • Evidence capture and readiness surfaced before release
  • Mobile execution subset for on-the-go actions instead of desktop-only assumptions
Mobile execution

Compact work lane, not a fake desktop clone

The mobile shell carries queue truth, quick tally, and next-action states without pretending the whole desk belongs on a phone.

Evidence posture

Documents stay attached to the assignment state

Evidence slots, finalized files, optional uploads, and submission posture should stay readable in one board instead of breaking the review trail.

Report workflow

Preview, QC, and tariff posture stay on one desk

Report generation works best when preview readiness, tariff class, generation-group truth, and QC status stay in one governed workspace.

Commercial clarity

Billing truth sits inside the product story

Credits, invoice events, free reruns, and plan readiness need to stay visible where teams decide whether work can proceed or must wait.

Report generation

Assisted report generation without pretending it is simple.

Assisted report generation is not marketed here as magic. It is presented as a reporting workflow with processing, review, revision, release, and commercial implications.

  • Tenant-side commissioning and clearer processing steps
  • Approval and finalization tied to real readiness
  • Future tariff-aware charging instead of flat beta-era claims
Governance

Studio and tenant roles stay visible but distinct.

Core tenant, approved tenant, operators, reviewers, tenant admins, associates, and studio users do not need the same UI or language. The public site now reflects that instead of flattening everyone into one generic story.

  • Reports require approval before release
  • Studio oversight kept separate from tenant-side work
  • Associate access stays narrower than internal team workflows

What Maulya prevents

Operational drift across intake, upload, approval, invoice follow-up, and release. The platform is most useful where teams currently lose time to unclear ownership and quiet backlog growth.

What Maulya clarifies

Whether the next blocker is workflow state, capability state, billing posture, or tenant approval state. The product story now matches that more serious truth.
