Terms

The public terms for the Maulya site, and the product rules that matter before you start.

Using this site

maulya.in is an informational site: the product, pricing, the blog, the survey and the way in. Reading it does not create an account or give access to the Maulya app.

The app

Working in Maulya happens in the app at app.maulya.in: signing in, your firm's files, reports, billing and settings. The app's own terms apply there.

Informational material

Articles, pricing explanations and product notes are informational. They do not replace the professional judgment of a registered valuer and are not legal, financial or regulatory advice.

Assisted report generation

When you use assisted report generation (coming soon), Not Alone Studios or Maulya helps fill in the property details form from the data you provide. The completed form is presented to you for review and approval before any report is generated.

Once you approve and submit the form, the resulting valuation report and all details in it are your responsibility. Not Alone Studios does not verify the accuracy of property data, valuation calculations or any professional judgment. The valuer's signature on the report is the valuer's professional opinion, not the opinion of Not Alone Studios.

Reports and new versions

A report is counted the first time it is made. Making the same report again in a new version does not count again.

The borrower's name and the bank can't be changed on a file after its first report. A report with a different borrower or bank needs a new file.

Beta

During the beta, firms are set up with us and plans are agreed with us. Any beta allowances are shown inside the app and can change while the beta is active.

Changes

These terms can be updated as the product, pricing and onboarding evolve. The latest public version is published on this page.
