Terms

Terms visibility for the public site and linked product flows.

This page explains the public-facing terms for the Maulya marketing site and highlights the product rules that matter for signup, trial access, credits, and assisted report generation.

Static site: maulya.in App host: app.maulya.in Auth origin: auth.maulya.in

Public-site use

The Maulya public site is a static informational site for product overview, pricing, updates discovery, survey access, and onboarding entry. Reading the public site does not itself create a workspace account or grant access to the Maulya application.

Linked app flows

Operational use of Maulya happens on linked product hosts.

  • app.maulya.in owns signup, workspace access, billing, report generation, and the full updates reader
  • auth.maulya.in owns sign-in and browser authentication
  • maulya.in remains the static public site for product story, pricing, survey, and public trust

Informational material

Articles, updates, pricing explanations, and product notes are informational. They do not replace the professional judgment of a registered valuer and should not be treated as legal, financial, or regulatory advice.

Assisted report generation

When you use the assisted report generation service, Not Alone Studios or Maulya helps fill in the property details form based on the data you provide. The completed form is presented to you for review and approval before any report is generated.

Once you approve and submit the form, the resulting valuation report and all details within it are your responsibility. Not Alone Studios does not verify the accuracy of property data, valuation calculations, or any professional judgments. The valuer's signature on the report represents the valuer's professional opinion, not the opinion of Not Alone Studios.

Credits and regeneration

Report credits are consumed when a report is first generated. Regeneration of the same report after editing non-core fields does not consume additional credits.

Core fields, including borrower name, property address, and bank name, cannot be changed after the initial generation. To generate a report with different core fields, a new credit is required.

Beta onboarding terms

During the early-access beta period, signup and onboarding happen through the live Maulya application. Any temporary access allowances or beta onboarding entitlements shown during signup are controlled by the live app flow and can change while the beta is active.

Workflow survey completion may unlock follow-up onboarding or product research steps during the beta period. Any such beta entitlements are shown inside the application when they apply.

Beta access and waitlist

Displayed prices are early-access beta prices. Beta access is limited to the first 50 firms. When beta access is full, new firms move into the waitlist flow during onboarding.

Changes

These terms can be updated as the product, pricing, and onboarding flows evolve. The latest public-facing version will be published on this page.

Important

The public site is the front door. The app is where the live work happens.

Use the static site to understand the product and pricing. Use the linked app hosts for signup, sign-in, updates reading, and product use.
