About

Built by the people closest to the work.

Maulya did not start as a software idea. It started because valuation work was being run across WhatsApp threads, scattered documents, memory and repeated follow-up, and the people closest to that decided to build the system they wanted to use.

What we believe

Valuers do not need more hustle. They need better systems. Maulya's job is to help a valuation office know where every file stands, who owns the next step and what is pending, without replacing the valuer's professional judgment.
