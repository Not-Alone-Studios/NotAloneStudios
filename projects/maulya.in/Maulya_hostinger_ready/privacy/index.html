Privacy notice

Privacy visibility for the public site and linked product surfaces.

This page explains the privacy posture of the static public marketing site at maulya.in. It is not a substitute for workspace-specific or updates-account policy acceptance that happens on the app host.

What this site covers

This notice covers the static public marketing and information site served from maulya.in and www.maulya.in, including product pages, pricing framing, updates discovery pages, and public pilot/contact entry points.

What this site does not cover

Operational workspace use, updates-account participation, comments, and sign-in flows are handled on separate Maulya product hosts, primarily app.maulya.in and auth.maulya.in. Those surfaces may require their own current policy acceptance inside the product.

Public site data

  • basic visit and hosting metadata required to serve the site
  • form submissions you explicitly send through public pilot or access flows
  • navigation into app-host or auth-host surfaces when you choose to continue

Updates and participation

Public reading can begin from this site, but article reading, comments, and updates-account management currently live on the app host. Comments and related participation are informational in nature and can require sign-in plus current policy acceptance.

Contact and questions

If you need clarity about which surface applies to you, use the pilot or associate-access entry points first so we can guide you into the correct lane.

Important

Authoritative policy acceptance may happen on the app host.

The marketing site is intentionally lightweight. If you are using the workspace or public updates-account/comment flows, the current in-product policy surfaces on app.maulya.in remain the authoritative interaction point.
