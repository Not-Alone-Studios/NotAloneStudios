Privacy notice

Privacy visibility for the public site and linked product hosts.

This page explains the privacy posture of the static public marketing site at maulya.in. It is not a substitute for app-specific or updates-account policy acceptance that happens on the app host.

What this site covers

This notice covers the static public marketing and information site served from maulya.in and www.maulya.in, including product pages, workflow survey entry, updates discovery pages, and public pilot/contact entry points.

What this site does not cover

Product use, updates-account participation, comments, and sign-in flows are handled on separate Maulya hosts, primarily app.maulya.in and auth.maulya.in. Those hosts may require their own current policy acceptance inside the product.

Public site data

  • basic visit and hosting metadata required to serve the site
  • survey submissions you explicitly send through the public workflow survey
  • form submissions you explicitly send through public pilot or access flows
  • navigation into the app host or auth host when you choose to continue

Updates and participation

Public reading can begin from this site, but article reading, comments, and updates-account management currently live on the app host. Comments and related participation are informational in nature and can require sign-in plus current policy acceptance.

Contact and questions

If you need clarity about which host applies to you, use the survey, pilot, or associate-access entry points first so we can guide you into the correct path.

Important

Authoritative policy acceptance may happen on the app host.

The marketing site is intentionally lightweight. If you are using the app or public updates-account/comment flows, the current in-product policy on app.maulya.in remains the authoritative interaction point.
