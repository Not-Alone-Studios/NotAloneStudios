Solutions and lanes

Different roles. Clearer responsibility. Less confusion.

Maulya is designed so valuation firms do not operate through one overloaded interface for everyone. Each role gets a more appropriate operating surface and clearer workflow language.

Open the app Request a pilot Associate lane
Firm owners

Control growth without operational mess.

For leaders who need clearer assignment visibility, team accountability, standardized workflows, and more confidence in report operations.

  • Operational visibility across teams
  • Fewer hidden blockers and follow-ups
  • More consistent delivery and commercial awareness
Tenant admins

Assign work, manage people, and understand what is blocked.

Tenant admins need more than a member list. They need approval context, onboarding visibility, role boundaries, and a clearer read on work that needs attention.

  • Role-aware access and invitation posture
  • Approval-needed vs ready states
  • Commercial posture that can be explained
Operators and reviewers

Know the next action without digging.

Ideal for teams that currently lose time to unclear ownership, evidence bottlenecks, repeated requests, and scattered review context.

  • Queue-first workflow
  • Evidence and review progression
  • Cleaner handoff flow
Field and associate roles

External users should not need the full internal machine.

Field assistants, valuers, and associates care about clarity, not governance complexity. Maulya gives them narrower onboarding and work surfaces so they can request, upload, respond, and track status without confusion.

  • Controlled join-request and access flow
  • Upload and clarification paths that feel simpler than the full workspace
  • Notifications, payment proof, and profile/help lanes where appropriate
Public readers

Knowledge is part of trust, but it stays separate from operations.

The marketing site introduces Maulya and its public knowledge lane. The actual updates reader, comments, public account settings, and contributor flows live on the app host so the product and participation rules stay in one place.

  • Public reading without entering the workspace
  • Auth-required participation when needed
  • Clear distinction between public reader and workspace user
