Plans and value framing

Plans built around operational use and report-generation usage.

Maulya combines platform access with report-generation usage posture. Plans can include monthly credits, and report workflows can vary by class and mode. The goal is to make usage more understandable, not less.

Important note

We are not publishing fake final pricing. Public numbers stay limited until plan entitlements, included-credit posture, tariff classes, and generation-mode packaging are stable enough to be honest. This page explains the shape of the commercial model instead.

Platform access

Role-aware workspace access

Base plans are expected to cover tenant access, role-aware operations, and the core workflow surfaces firms need to run assignments with more clarity.

  • Tenant admin, operator, reviewer, and field-facing roles
  • Assignment, evidence, and workflow progression surfaces
  • Public updates and reader entry as a separate lane
Credits

Included monthly credits plus add-on posture

The current direction is to make included credits visible, distinguish them from purchased credits, and surface their effect where billable actions happen.

  • Monthly included credits
  • Purchased credits where needed
  • Blocked states that explain whether the issue is plan or credit posture
Report usage

Tariff-aware generation, not flat guesswork

Report-generation usage is moving toward tariff-aware charging with clearer treatment of standard and assisted paths, instead of one flat credit story that hides the real product behavior.

  • Standard and assisted workflow support
  • Tariff-aware report classes
  • Regeneration-aware usage logic
Why we frame it this way

Commercial clarity should reduce confusion, not create it.

Valuation firms do not need fake simplicity. They need to understand what is included, what changes by report class or generation mode, and what gets blocked or charged under which conditions.

  • Clearer plan posture
  • Included monthly credits where applicable
  • Commercial cues attached to report-generation workflows
Safe public wording

What we can say today

  • Platform access is packaged with operational use in mind
  • Credits and report-generation posture are part of the product story
  • Exact price grids remain intentionally deferred until packaging is honest
