Revalk

Earlier work · 2026 · no longer in development

A calm study lab for NEET PG and INI-CET aspirants.

Revalk was a study product the studio built in early 2026. It is kept here as part of the studio’s record of work.

What it was

From a doubt to recall.

Revalk turned confusion into something you could revise. You explored concepts in topic spaces, collected the ones that mattered into a private Vault, and each saved concept became a small Walk Pack: a few flashcards, a trap note and a short audio recap.

Then came the review loop: recall under a little pressure, a retest before the memory dropped, and a view of what was sticking and what was not.

Concepts were tiered from Iron to Platinum by how close they sat to past exam topics: a relevance rank, not a quality rank. It was built for long, quiet sessions on a tablet, with no feed and no chat.
