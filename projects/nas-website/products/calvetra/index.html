Calvetra

In development

Plan your preparation from what rankers, faculty and students say matters.

Calvetra v2 is three things: the Library, the Planner and the Topic Board, for NEET PG and INI-CET aspirants, on a model that works for any exam.

Where it stands

In development, built slowly, by choice.

The design is finished as a clickable prototype of 84 screens. The building has started with the database’s foundation and the syllabus’s topic tree. Calvetra isn’t open to students yet.

The four ideas

Blunt about the data, warm about the person.

01 · The verdict

One honest sentence, then see what to drop.

Every plan says whether it fits, in one sentence, always “about”, always marked as an estimate: “Fits, with about 12 days to spare.” or “About 66 hours short.”

When it’s short, the first button is See what to drop: the lowest-rated topics in your plan, each with its voices, which you can untick. Adding time comes second. The last 10 days are always revision.

02 · Anyone’s plan

Anyone’s plan, fitted to your months.

Adopt a ranker’s ten-month plan as an intern with 24 hours a week, and Calvetra fits it to your dates, then lists exactly what had to give: lower-rated topics moved to If time allows, a fourth revision dropped.

Their real data is labelled “self-reported” or “tracked in Calvetra”, never “verified”. A ranker’s plan is a resource you use, like a book.

03 · The Topic Board

Voices side by side, never blended.

Every topic shows who says it matters, in one fixed order, each voice on its own:

  • Rankers
  • Faculty
  • Students
  • Past exams, once cleared

Counts below 10 read “7 of 9”; percentages start at 10 (“61% of 512”). The Board’s order is a formula printed on the page.

04 · Freeform

Freeform, with undo, inside a routine.

Your day is your routine’s slots: “07:00–09:00 · New study”. Drag anything anywhere and it’s pinned. The planner never moves a pin; it makes room by moving something else, and says what. Every change shows Undo.

The coach is eight fixed rules with published thresholds, not AI. Each card shows its numbers and its source, with Not now, Never for this, and “why?”.

Every picture on this page is from Calvetra’s design prototype. Names and numbers are invented.

Three places

Plan, Topics, Library.

Plan

What to study now, and whether it all fits. Today, Week, Whole plan and Routine. Plan opens on Today, because the daily list is the reason to open the app.

Topics

The Topic Board: each subject’s topics, rated by endorsed rankers, faculty and students, each shown separately. Every topic is one tap from a plan.

Library

Platforms, faculty, books and test series, and people’s plans, routines and agendas. Facts first, then voices, then counts, on every page.

What it won’t do

Nothing is sold by fear.

Short sentences a tired intern can read at 2 a.m.

  • No countdowns, no streaks, no “others are ahead of you”.
  • No rank prediction and no leaderboard.
  • No red for a shortfall. Red is only for Delete.
  • Money never buys position: platforms can’t rate anything, and can’t pay to be placed higher.
  • Every number says where it comes from, in words, next to it.
